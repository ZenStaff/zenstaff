## A new, very fast colleague

An AI agent is a program you talk to in plain language that can also act: it reads files, writes new ones, edits documents and, if your company allows it, works in apps like Google Drive.

Think of it as a new colleague who never gets tired. It knows a lot in general, and nothing about your company until you tell it.

:::picture chat
A thread: you write what you need, the agent shows what it does, step by step.
:::

## Thread and folder

Two words you will see everywhere in Zen Staff:

- **Thread**: one conversation with the agent, for one task. The agent remembers what you said earlier in the same thread.
- **Folder**: the place where the agent works. It sees the files in it and saves its results there.

:::picture folder
The agent only sees what is in the folder or in a connected app.
:::

:::tip
Want it to work on the price list? Put the file in the folder first. If the file is not there, the agent cannot read it and may guess instead.
:::

## How to start a task

You have two ways:

1. **A card on the Office home.** Press release, Weekly report, Client offer email and more. The card asks you a few things in the chat, then starts with good instructions already written.
2. **A free message**, written the way you would write to a colleague.

:::picture office-cards
On the Office home, every card is a task that is ready to go.
:::

:::example A good free message
"Read sedinta.md and make me a list of who does what, with deadlines."
:::

Start with cards. They teach you what a good request looks like.

## How to stop it

While it works you see what it is doing, step by step. If it goes the wrong way, press **Stop**. Nothing breaks: you write what you actually wanted and continue in the same thread.

:::picture stop
Stop is always at hand while the agent works.
:::

:::example When you only want it to look
"Do not change any file, just tell me what you see." Use this sentence a lot in your first week.
:::

## You stay responsible

The agent writes confidently even when it is wrong. It can invent a number, a date or a name that sounds right.

:::dont
You paste the agent's summary straight into the email to the client.
:::

:::do
You read the result, check the numbers against the original file, and only then send it.
:::

The agent saves you hours of typing. The judgment stays with you.

## Try it now

:::try
You open a practice folder with three small files: a meeting note, a product list and a to-do list. The agent describes them in 5 lines and suggests 3 things it could do, without changing anything. Pick one of its suggestions and let it work.
:::

:::quiz
The agent is going the wrong way. What do you do?

- [ ] Close the app and start over.
- [x] Press Stop and write what you actually wanted, in the same thread.
- [ ] Wait for it to finish, it might turn out fine.

> Stop breaks nothing. The agent stops and you continue the thread with a clearer instruction.
:::

:::quiz
The agent cannot find the price list. What is the most likely reason?

- [x] The file is not in the thread's folder.
- [ ] The agent cannot read spreadsheets.
- [ ] You need to ask more politely.

> The agent only sees the files in the folder it works in and the connected apps.
:::

:::quiz
Who is responsible for an email sent with text written by the agent?

- [ ] The agent.
- [ ] The company that made the app.
- [x] You, the person who sent it.

> Whatever leaves your desk is your work. That is why you read and check first.
:::
