## Why use the cards

The Office page has a **Press release** card and a **Social media post set** card. Each asks you the questions a PR person would ask.

Then it gives the agent instructions that already follow the usual structure: headline, lead, body, quote, the "About us" paragraph, contact.

Your job is to fill the fields with real facts. The quality of what you put in is the quality you get out.

:::picture office-cards
The Press release and Social media post set cards are on the Office home.
:::

## The news, the company and the quote

- **News.** One or two sentences: who does what, when, where.
- **Organization.** Two lines about your company: what it is, since when, what it does. This becomes the "About us" paragraph.
- **Quote person.** A real person, their role, and the words they actually said or approved.

:::dont
"An exciting new chapter."
:::

:::do
"Brutăria Spic opens its second shop on 1 December in Gheorgheni."
:::

:::warn
If you leave the quote field vague, the agent writes a quote nobody said, and journalists print it under that person's name. Get their approval in writing before you send.
:::

## The numbers and the contact details

- **Key facts.** Numbers and specifics: address, date, hours, how many jobs, how much was invested, what is special. Journalists use facts with numbers. Adjectives get deleted.
- **Contact.** Name, role, email and phone of the person who answers the press. Check them twice.
- **City and date.** The dateline, for example "Cluj-Napoca, 20 November 2026".
- **Source URL.** A page with more details, if you have one.

:::tip
Leave the source URL empty rather than put in an invented one.
:::

## The post set: the extra choices

- **Audience** and **networks**: "parents in Cluj, Instagram + Facebook" gives different posts from "local business community, LinkedIn".
- **Tone** and **number of posts**: start with 3. Fewer posts, better checked.
- **Call to action**: one concrete thing, not "follow us for more".

:::example A good call to action
"Come on 1 December before 12 for free coffee."
:::

The card writes a version for each network. Instagram gets shorter text with hashtags, LinkedIn something more serious, Facebook something in between. Still read every one: a joke that works on Instagram may not work on LinkedIn.

## Review before you send

1. Every fact in the release appears in what you filled in. Anything new (a number, a partner, a "first in Romania") gets checked or removed.
2. The quote is word for word the approved one.
3. Names, address, date and phone are correct.
4. The headline says the news, not a slogan.

:::example The question that catches what was invented
"List every fact in the release and where you got it from." Anything without a source goes.
:::
