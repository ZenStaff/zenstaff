# Contract review in plain language

The owner should finish reading knowing: what they commit to, what can go wrong and how badly,
what is unusual, and exactly what to ask or change before signing. Quote the contract, never
paraphrase it into something it does not say.

## The review file, in this order

1. **First line**: `**Nu e consultanță juridică.**` followed by one sentence: this is a
   plain-language reading to prepare the discussion; for large amounts, long commitments or
   anything in "Când chemi un avocat", a lawyer reads it before signing. (English report:
   `**This is not legal advice.**`)
2. **Pe scurt** (5-8 lines): who signs with whom, what each one gives, the price and how it is
   paid, how long it lasts and how it renews, how either side can leave, and the overall
   verdict: `Se poate semna` / `Se poate semna după clarificări` / `Nu semna în forma asta`.
3. **Termenii principali**: a table Clause | What it says (short quote, at most 25 words) |
   What it means for us. Price, payment term, delivery/acceptance, duration, renewal,
   termination, penalties, liability, confidentiality, data protection, disputes and
   applicable law, annexes.
4. **Riscuri**, highest first. Each: level (mare / mediu / mic), the clause number and a short
   quote, what could happen in money or time, and how to reduce it (a change to ask for, with
   suggested wording marked "Propunere de formulare").
5. **Clauze neobișnuite**: what is unusual for this kind of contract between Romanian
   businesses (or with consumers), and why it matters. Typical examples: automatic renewal
   with a long notice, termination only for one side, penalties for one side only or above
   the value of the contract, liability unlimited for us and capped for them, payment terms
   over 60 days between businesses, exclusivity, non-compete, transfer of intellectual
   property, jurisdiction abroad, one side may change prices or terms alone.
6. **Ce lipsește**: clauses a contract of this kind normally has and this one does not (for
   example: a way out, a cap on liability, a data processing agreement when personal data is
   shared, acceptance criteria, what happens with the deposit).
7. **Întrebări pentru cealaltă parte**: numbered, ready to send.
8. **Când chemi un avocat**: only the triggers that apply here (amount, duration, guarantees
   or personal guarantees, real estate, employment, intellectual property, a foreign law or
   court, anything you could not understand).

## Romanian law: say only what you are sure of

- Standard clauses that limit liability, allow one side to terminate or suspend, cut the other
  side's rights, impose a forfeiture, restrict trade with third parties, renew the contract
  tacitly, or choose a court/arbitration take effect only if expressly accepted in writing
  (clauze neuzuale, Codul civil art. 1203).
- Between businesses, payment terms longer than 60 days need express agreement and must not
  be abusive (Legea 72/2013); late payment interest applies by law.
- Penalty clauses (clauza penală) are valid; a court may reduce an obviously excessive one.
- Anything else legal: write it only if you are certain, with the act and article;
  otherwise phrase it as a question for a lawyer and mark [CHECK].

## Rules

- Quote clause numbers and words exactly; if the contract has no numbering, quote the heading.
- Never say a clause is "illegal" or "void": say it may be contested or unusual and why.
- Never add facts about the other party; never assess the person's own obligations as fine
  when the contract does not say so.
- Plain words: explain any legal term the first time in brackets.
- Romanian with ș and ț (comma below). No emoji.

## Self-review (do it, then fix what fails)

1. The file starts with the "Nu e consultanță juridică" line (or the English one).
2. Every risk and unusual clause cites a clause and quotes it; no quote longer than 25 words.
3. The verdict matches the risks (a "mare" risk without a fix means "Nu semna în forma asta"
   or "după clarificări").
4. Every legal statement is one from the list above or carries the act and article or [CHECK].
5. Questions are concrete and can be sent as they are.
6. Nothing in the contract was skipped: annexes are covered or named as missing.
