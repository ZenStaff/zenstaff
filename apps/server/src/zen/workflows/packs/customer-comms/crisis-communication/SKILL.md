# Crisis communication: the first hours

Speak early, say what you know and what you are doing, show you care about the people
affected, promise only what you control, and say when you will update. Silence or a defensive
post makes most small crises bigger.

## assessment.md (internal, one page)

- **Severity**: low (a few angry comments), medium (spreading, local press asking), high
  (health or safety, a person hurt, authorities involved, legal exposure). One line why.
- **Facts / unknowns**: two lists, from the form only.
- **Who hears it first**: people affected directly, employees (before they read it online),
  authorities when the law requires it (food safety: DSVSA; data breach: ANSPDCP within 72
  hours; work accident: ITM), insurer, then the public. Mark the legal ones "[CHECK] confirm
  with your lawyer", and do not state deadlines other than the ones written here.
- **Do not say yet**: guesses about the cause, blame (staff, supplier, customer), numbers that
  are not confirmed, anything about a person's health or identity, "no comment".
- **Legal and insurance**: when there is injury, illness, a data breach or a claim, the
  statement is read by a lawyer before it goes out if one can be reached within the hour;
  meanwhile the holding statement below avoids admitting legal liability while still
  apologising for what people went through.

## holding-statement.md (public, 80-150 words)

What happened in one sentence of fact; that you are sorry for what the people affected went
through (empathy, not legal liability); what you have done and are doing now; what people
should do if they are affected (a phone or email from the form; never invented); when the next
update comes (a time). Signed by the spokesperson. Variants: a short version for social media
(under 60 words) and a version for the website or a pinned post. No jargon, no "regretăm
orice neplăcere", no passive voice that hides who acts.

## qa.md (for the spokesperson)

12-20 likely questions, the hard ones included (Why did it happen? Was anyone hurt? Will you
compensate? Is it safe now? Did you hide it? Who is responsible?), each with a short truthful
answer from the facts, the bridge line when the answer is not known yet ("We do not know yet;
we will say by ..."), and what never to say. Separate sections for customers, press and
employees.

## social-replies.md

Reply set for the comments: a first public reply (the short statement), replies for: angry
customer, worried customer, someone affected (move to private message / phone at once, no
details in public), a rumour or false claim (correct calmly with facts, once), a journalist in
the comments (direct to the spokesperson's contact), a troll (no reply, or one factual reply,
then stop; hide only insults, never criticism). Each 1-3 sentences, human, no copy-paste feel:
give 2 wordings for the frequent ones. Rules for the team: reply within 1-2 hours during the
day, never argue, never delete criticism, never post from personal accounts.

## next-24-hours.md

A timeline: now / +1 h / +4 h / end of day / tomorrow morning, with who does what (from the
roles in the form), what to monitor (comments, reviews, mentions), when to update, and what
would raise the severity (and then: lawyer, authorities, a fuller statement).

## Rules

- Every fact comes from the form. Unknown facts are said as unknown, with when you will know.
- Apologise for the experience, not for a cause that is not established.
- Never offer money, vouchers or compensation in public unless the form says it was decided.
- Romanian with ș and ț (comma below); no emoji; plain words.

## Self-review (do it, then fix what fails)

1. No invented fact, cause, number or promise anywhere.
2. The holding statement has: fact, empathy, action, what to do, next update time, signature;
   80-150 words; the short version under 60 words.
3. Nothing admits legal liability or blames anyone; health, data and accident cases point to
   the lawyer and the authority.
4. Q&A covers the hard questions with truthful bridge answers.
5. Social replies move affected people to private channels and never share personal details.
