# Daily menu texts and posts

People decide lunch in a few seconds while scrolling, around 10:30-12:00. The post must say
what, how much, where, until when, in the first lines, and make them hungry without lying.

## Exactness first

- Copy dish names, quantities (grams, ml) and prices exactly as given. Do not add dishes,
  sides, drinks, discounts or "fresh/homemade/local" claims the menu does not state.
- Price format: "39 lei". Hours as given ("12:00-16:00", "până se termină").
- Allergens: when given, write them per dish (the 14 EU allergens: gluten, crustaceans, eggs,
  fish, peanuts, soy, milk, nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites, lupin, molluscs). When
  not given, add one line: "Pentru informații despre alergeni, întreabă-ne." (EU rules require
  restaurants to give allergen information on request); never guess them.
- A dish that is vegetarian or post (Orthodox fasting) only if the menu says so.

## Per channel (only the channels chosen)

- **Facebook**: a first line with the hook (the dish of the day or the full menu at a price),
  the menu as a short list (felul 1, felul 2, desert), price, serving hours, address or
  delivery, how to order. 40-90 words. 2-3 local hashtags at most (#prânz #<oraș>).
- **Instagram**: caption 30-70 words, the list, then 3-6 hashtags; plus **3 story frames**
  (frame 1: the photo with "Prânzul de azi · 39 lei"; frame 2: the full menu; frame 3: how to
  order with a link or "trimite-ne mesaj").
- **WhatsApp** (status and broadcast list): 25-50 words, the list on separate lines, price,
  hours, phone or "răspunde la mesaj ca să rezervi". Broadcast lists only reach people who
  saved the number and agreed to receive messages.
- **Google Business Profile** update: 40-80 words, menu and price, with the button suggestion
  ("Comandă online" / "Sună"); no phone numbers or links inside the text (Google may reject the
  post).
- **Menu board / printed sheet** (`menu-board.md`): title, date, the courses with grams and
  price, hours, allergens line, nothing else. Large, simple, readable from three meters.

For a whole week: one block per day (date from the form; weekdays checked with `date` or the
calendar, never from memory), plus `plan.md` with the best posting time per channel and one
line per day to vary the hook (Monday: "start the week", Friday: the special) without
repeating the same opening.

## Words

Concrete and sensory, from the dish itself ("ciorbă de perișoare cu leuștean", "piept de pui
la grătar cu piure de cartofi") rather than "delicios" and "savuros" in every line. No
exclamation marks in a row, no emoji, no capital letters for whole words. Romanian with ș and ț
(comma below).

## Self-review (do it, then fix what fails)

1. Every dish, quantity, price and hour matches the menu given, letter for letter, and every
   channel's list has the whole menu, including what is included (drink, bread, side).
2. Each channel's text is within its length and has what, price, hours, how to order.
3. Allergens are the given ones or the generic line, never guessed.
4. No phone or link inside the Google Business Profile text.
5. Dates and weekdays are right; no hook repeated across the week.
