# Follow-up sequence after a meeting

Each email must be worth opening on its own: it moves the deal one step, adds something useful
or makes saying "no" easy. No "just checking in".

## The sequence (3 or 4 emails)

1. **Recap, same day or next morning**: thanks in one line; what you understood (their goal,
   their constraint, in their words from the notes); what you agreed; the next step with a
   date and who does what; the promised materials attached or linked. 90-150 words.
2. **Value, 3-4 working days later**: one useful thing tied to a point from the meeting (an
   answer to a question they asked, an example, a short calculation from their numbers, a
   relevant case only if the materials include it); one easy question. 60-110 words.
3. **Decision help, about a week later**: make the choice simple (two options, or the one
   change that would make it work for them), mention a real constraint only if it is true
   (availability, a date in the notes). 60-100 words.
4. **Polite close, 10-14 days later** (when the form asks for 4): "Should I close this for now?"
   style; leave the door open; no guilt. 40-70 words.

For each: send day (relative to the meeting and as a date when the meeting date is known,
skipping weekends), subject (under 50 characters, specific, no "Re:" tricks, no "Follow-up"),
preview line, body, signature, and **If they reply**: what to do next in one or two lines.

## crm-notes.md

The meeting in five lines, decision makers and roles (only as named in the notes), budget and
timing if mentioned, objections, open points, next step and date.

## Rules

- Only facts from the notes, the offer and the materials. No invented discounts, deadlines,
  "only 2 spots left", statistics or testimonials; unknowns are [CHECK: ...].
- Write like the sender talks: short sentences, "dumneavoastră" or "tu" as in the notes
  (default "dumneavoastră" in Romanian B2B). No emoji, no exclamation marks in subjects.
- Every email has exactly one ask.
- Respect: if the notes say they will decide after a date, the sequence waits for it.
- Romanian with ș and ț (comma below).

## Self-review (do it, then fix what fails)

1. Email 1 restates what was agreed and the next step with a date.
2. Each email adds something new and has one clear ask; none says only "checking in".
3. Lengths and send days follow the plan; the dates skip weekends.
4. Nothing invented; [CHECK] marks for unknowns.
5. Subjects are specific and under 50 characters.
