# A newsletter people open, read in two minutes and act on

## The file, in this order

1. **Subject plan and A/B test**
   - Variant A and variant B: two subject lines that differ in **one** thing only (benefit vs
     curiosity, a number vs none, the offer named vs the story), each under 45 characters,
     each with its own preview text under 90 characters that adds to the subject instead of
     repeating it. Say in one line what the test learns.
   - The test plan, sized to the list (from the form; when not given, write it for "up to 2,000"
     and "more than 2,000"):
     - under 1,000 contacts: no winner round; send A to half and B to half and keep the lesson
       for next time;
     - 1,000 to 5,000: 20% get A, 20% get B, the winner goes to the other 60% after 4 hours;
     - more than 5,000: 10% and 10%, winner after 2-4 hours.
       Decide the winner on **clicks** (or click rate), not opens: Apple Mail and others open
       emails automatically, so opens are inflated. Name where this is set in common tools
       (Mailchimp: A/B test campaign; Brevo: A/B testing; MailerLite: A/B split campaign)
       without claiming exact menu names.
   - When the form says no test: put the subject you recommend first, keep B as the
     alternative, skip the test plan.
   - 3 spare subject lines for next time.
2. **The issue**: a short intro (2-3 sentences, a person talking), one section per update
   (headline, 2-4 sentences, a link written as [LINK: what it opens]), the main call to
   action as one button label (2-4 words, a verb), and a friendly sign-off with a real name
   when one is known. Readable in two minutes: about 250-400 words.
3. **Per segment** (when segments were given): a table "what changes" (intro line, the order of
   the sections, the button, anything left out), then for each segment the intro and button
   ready to paste. Keep the facts identical; only emphasis and wording change. Never write
   anything that reveals what you know about a person ("we saw you bought...").
4. **Plain-text version** of the main issue (no formatting), for the text part of the email.
5. **Before sending**: test send to yourself on a phone; every [LINK] replaced; unsubscribe
   link present; only people who agreed to receive it (GDPR); sender name the business, not
   "noreply"; send time suggestion for this audience.

## Rules

- Every price, date, offer and claim comes from the updates or the facts; otherwise
  [CHECK: ...]. No fake deadlines or "last chance" without a real end date.
- No spam words in subjects ("GRATUIT!!!", "100%", "urgent", all caps, many "!"), no emoji in
  the documents.
- Romanian with ș and ț (comma below).

## Self-review (do it, then fix what fails)

1. A and B differ in exactly one element; both under 45 characters (count them); previews
   under 90.
2. The test plan matches the list size and picks the winner on clicks.
3. Every segment version keeps the same facts and offer as the main issue.
4. No invented numbers or offers; every link is [LINK: ...] or a real URL from the input.
5. The issue is under about 400 words and has exactly one main button.
