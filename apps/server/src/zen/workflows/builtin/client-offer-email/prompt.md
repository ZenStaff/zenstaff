Client: {{client}}

Look in this folder for price lists, previous offers, case studies or terms and conditions, and use them for consistency. Never change a price given above; never invent discounts.

Write offer-email.md with:

1. Three subject line options (under 8 words, specific).
2. The email: open with their need in their words, then the solution in 3 to 5 bullet points focused on outcomes, then the price stated clearly (what is included, what is not, VAT), then one proof point if the folder has one, then ONE clear next step with a date.
3. A short version (under 80 words) for when the client prefers brief messages.
4. A polite follow-up email to send 3 working days later if there is no answer.
5. A list of anything you assumed, marked [CHECK].

Keep paragraphs to 2 or 3 lines. No "I hope this email finds you well".

When you finish, reply with a short summary. For each text file you wrote (.md, .docx, .xlsx and similar), give one line on what it holds and its full absolute path. For each image, audio or video file you made, embed it in the reply as `![short title](/absolute/path/to/file.ext)` so the person can see or play it right in the chat.
