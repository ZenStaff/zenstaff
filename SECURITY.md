# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately to **security@zenstaff.app** (or with GitHub's "Report a
vulnerability" button on this repository). Do not open a public issue.

Include the version (Settings > About), your operating system, what is affected and how to
reproduce it. We confirm receipt within 3 working days and tell you what we will do and when. We
credit reporters in the release notes unless you prefer not to be named.

A vulnerability that also affects upstream T3 Code: we report it to them as well, with your
permission. Supported versions: the latest stable and beta releases.
